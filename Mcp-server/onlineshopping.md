# What is Online Shopping?

Online shopping is a form of electronic commerce that allows consumers to directly purchase goods or services from a seller over the internet. This is typically done using a web browser or a mobile application.

## Key aspects of online shopping include:

*   **Convenience:** Shoppers can browse and purchase items from anywhere, at any time, without needing to visit a physical store.
*   **Process:** It involves searching for products, adding them to a virtual shopping cart, making electronic payments, and then receiving the items via delivery or digital download.
*   **Platforms:** Online shopping occurs through various digital platforms such as online shops, e-stores, virtual stores, webshops, or internet stores.
*   **Information:** Online stores often provide detailed product descriptions, photos, multimedia files, specifications, and customer reviews to help consumers make informed decisions.
